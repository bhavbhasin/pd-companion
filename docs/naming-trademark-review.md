# Kampa — naming & trademark review

**Reviewed 2026-07-30. Verdict: keep the name. No evidence of a blocking conflict in our classes.**
Not a legal clearance — see "What is NOT verified" before spending money on a filing.

## The name

- App / product: **Kampa**. Domain **kampa.health** (live). Backup **kampavata.com**.
- Our classes: **Nice 9** (software) and **Nice 44** (medical/health services).
- "Kampavata" is the Ayurvedic term for Parkinson's — origin of the earlier working name.

## The one registration we confirmed

**KAMPA** — US Reg. **7058070**, Serial **90330383**
- Owner: Dometic Sweden AB, Hemvärnsgatan 15, Solna, Sweden
- Filed 2020-11-19, registered 2023-05-23, filing basis intent-to-use. **Status: live.**
- Goods: electric air pumps for inflating air beds / mattresses / inflatable furniture / tents /
  awnings / windbreaks; electric pumps; power drill bits and non-electrical drill adapters;
  electric and power jacks; mechanical winders; structural parts and fittings for the above.

Those goods are **Class 7 machinery** (one aggregator also indicated a floor-coverings class,
Class 27, consistent with Kampa's awning carpets). **Neither is Class 9 or Class 44.**

⚠️ This corrects an earlier working assumption that Dometic occupied Class 9 because it sells
connected electronics. The registration we found does not support that. Electric air pumps are
Class 7, not Class 9.

## Risk assessment

**Dometic — low.** Different classes, different goods, different trade channels. No plausible
consumer confusion between a caravan awning pump and a Parkinson's symptom tracker. They are
well-lawyered, so this is the party most *capable* of objecting, but on these goods they have
thin grounds.

**Kampa Health Products (Tanzania) — very low.** Trademark rights are territorial; theirs sit in
Tanzania. Reaching us would need US/UK/EU registration or a Madrid Protocol extension, and nothing
in their profile suggests either. 15 outbound shipments total; shipping physical goods into the US
does not create US trademark rights in a name. Their goods are Classes 5/10/35 (physical medical
supplies and wholesale), not 9/44. ⛔ **A Dun & Bradstreet page confers no rights** — D&B is a
business-credit directory built from company registries and customs data, not a trademark register.

**Crowded field — cuts both ways.** Multiple unrelated parties coexist under "Kampa" across
camping, housing, medical wholesale and design. That weakens anyone's claim to broad exclusivity,
including ours. Our enforceable asset is more likely the composite **"Kampa Health"** plus the
wordmark/logo than the bare word "Kampa".

**"Health Products" / ".health" are descriptive** and carry little weight. The distinctive element
on both sides is "Kampa" alone.

## What is NOT verified

Register search was done indirectly (search engines + aggregators). USPTO TESS, EUIPO eSearch and
UKIPO are JavaScript applications and could not be queried directly; Justia returned HTTP 403.

Specifically unknown:
- Whether **any** party holds KAMPA in **Class 9 or Class 44** in the US, EU or UK.
- Dometic's full EU/UK class coverage.
- The Tanzanian register.

Absence of evidence here is weak evidence of absence. Before spending on a filing, or before any
raise/acquisition diligence, buy a real clearance search (~$300–1,500).

## Actions

   the only item the 2-week launch timeline actually threatens.

the live site, the brand guide, App Store metadata, the social presence, and outbound mail already
sent carrying kampa.health.
